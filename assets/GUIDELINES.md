# EasyAI — logo usage

## The mark

Two halves of one block, joined through a stepped seam. The orange half is
the software you add; the ink half is the system it joins. The two-tone split
keeps the idea readable at small sizes.

- Grid: 256 × 256, block 176 × 176, radius 40, seam 20.
- Built with real geometry (no live text, rasters, filters or gradients).
- Tested at 16, 32, 48, 64, 256 px, in one colour, reversed and mirrored.

## Colour

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand | Signal Orange | `#EB5A2E` | 235, 90, 46 |
| Ink | Ink | `#111111` | 17, 17, 17 |
| Paper | White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 |

White on Signal Orange is 3.46:1 and Ink on Signal Orange is 5.45:1. Use Ink
for text on orange; white is for the mark and large graphics only.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- Clear space on every side = one seam width (`1/8` of the block).
- Mark: minimum 16 px digital, 6 mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: minimum 120 px wide, 35 mm print.
- Stacked lockup: minimum 72 px wide, 20 mm print.

## Backgrounds

- Primary mark: white, light neutrals, warm paper, photography.
- `easyai-mark-on-dark.svg`: dark surfaces and dark UI chrome.
- `easyai-mark-mono.svg` / `-mono-white.svg`: one-colour reproduction only.
  They lose the two-piece split below ~24 px.
- `favicon.svg` adapts to dark mode; use it for browser icons.

## Typography

The wordmark is **Noto Sans Bold** (SIL Open Font License), converted to
outlines. It is safe to use in a logo. For a longer-lived identity, license a
display face and re-outline the wordmark with the same tracking.

## Misuse

- Do not close, widen or reposition the seam.
- Do not re-colour one half only, or add a third colour.
- Do not rotate, skew, outline or add effects to the block.
- Do not rebuild the wordmark with a different weight or tracking.
- Do not place the mark on orange or a warm photo without a separating field.

## Before public release

This is a design deliverable, not trademark clearance. Run a trademark search
(EUIPO/USPTO/CNIPA) and a reverse image search before launch.
